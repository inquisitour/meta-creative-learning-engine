# Learnings for the Content team (hooks and CTAs)

Based on 80 ads, 4,720,596 impressions (each effect is vs the average level of its attribute, shown with a 95% confidence interval).

## Winner
- cta = gift_now drives more purchases per impression: +25.7% (95% CI +13.9% to +38.6%), across 18 ads. **Action:** Make 3 more variants in this direction.
- hook = social_proof drives more purchases per impression: +15.1% (95% CI +3.8% to +27.7%), across 31 ads. **Action:** Make 3 more variants in this direction.

## Loser
- hook = question underperforms: -15.2% (95% CI -27.1% to -1.4%), across 15 ads. **Action:** Stop producing this. Retire running ads that use it.
- cta = learn_more underperforms: -18.4% (95% CI -26.4% to -9.6%), across 27 ads. **Action:** Stop producing this. Retire running ads that use it.

## Inconclusive
- hook = offer_led: no reliable effect yet. Estimate +21.6% (95% CI -1.6% to +50.1%), 5 ads. **Action:** No decision yet. Keep it in rotation so the test can finish.
- cta = shop_now: no reliable effect yet. Estimate -2.5% (95% CI -10.5% to +6.3%), 35 ads. **Action:** No decision yet. Keep it in rotation so the test can finish.
- hook = problem_solution: no reliable effect yet. Estimate -4.4% (95% CI -16.8% to +9.9%), 18 ads. **Action:** No decision yet. Keep it in rotation so the test can finish.
- hook = story: no reliable effect yet. Estimate -11.9% (95% CI -24.1% to +2.2%), 11 ads. **Action:** No decision yet. Keep it in rotation so the test can finish.
